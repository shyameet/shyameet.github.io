## A separate strategy for each job

Not one strategy trying to do everything. Separate strategies for separate jobs:

- **Passing the eval** — a strategy that actually passes a prop firm evaluation in one
  day. Finding it is the work for the coming days.
- **Big money on the funded account** — once funded, a strategy for making a big amount.
- **The consistency rule** — a strategy that makes small amounts every day, to stay inside
  the funded account's consistency rule.
