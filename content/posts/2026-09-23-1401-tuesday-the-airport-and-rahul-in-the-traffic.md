Tuesday, 22 September. A lot of mistakes, and a very good day anyway.

The mistakes first. The gift for Rahul's parents, who were leaving for Canada, got left to
the very last day. Then I didn't plan getting to the airport properly and missed them.
Both are filed under Mistakes.

The good part: I kept my word and met Rahul, even after he had second thoughts about
meeting. When I got there he was standing in the middle of a traffic jam, just observing
it. Where he lives now there's none of this — the noise, the jams, the crowd. That only
happens in Mumbai. I was shocked — what is he doing in the middle of the road at this
time? He told me it was his past self. He used to be in that same seat, doing the same
job, commuting between home and the office.

We had coffee, and we formulated some new plans and ideas — they're in Goals. At the end
I told him I needed a tablet, and he said he'll gift it to me on my birthday, 22 October.

The plan for the coming days: find a new strategy that actually passes prop firm accounts
in one day.
