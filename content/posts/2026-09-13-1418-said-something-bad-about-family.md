---
title: "Said something bad about family, outside the family"
date: 2026-09-13T14:18:00+05:30
section: mistakes
lesson: "Never say bad things about family outside the family, no matter how desperate I feel in the moment."
tags: [people]
---

Talking to Patrick — honestly, because he is close and I trust him — I said something bad
about my own family. It was the truth as I saw it, and he needed the honesty. But family
stays inside the family. That rule does not bend just because I was desperate to say it to
someone in the moment.
