Rahul's parents were leaving for Canada, and I had so much time to plan a gift for them.
I still let it go — procrastinated until the very last day.

On that last day I had a very good idea: a crochet of Arthur and Mao, our beloved dog
and cat, made by my sister, so they'd have them with them in Canada. But it was too late
to make it.
