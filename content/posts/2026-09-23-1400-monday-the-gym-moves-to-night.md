Monday I was at home. I can't remember much else of it.

The one thing that changed: I've started going to the gym late at night, after taking
Rocco for his walk. The reason is the morning. My mind says that if I go first thing, I
waste the starting hour of the day — and for a mind like mine that first hour is crucial.
It's when most of the ideas should come.

I'm still not sure which is right: the gym in the first hour after I wake up, or late at
night, when I'm more energised and most of the work is already done. For the past few
days it's been the late-night session, and I'm really liking it — it was my old habit. But
it's not good for a lifestyle like mine. Leaving that aside for now.
