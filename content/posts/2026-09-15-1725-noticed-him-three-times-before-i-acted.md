Same incident as the win — the man at Dadar station. I'm still turning over two things:
whether I should have done more than push him off, and whether I should have gone after
him instead of letting my train be the reason to stop. I don't have a clean answer to
either one.

What I do know for certain is the part actually worth fixing: I clocked what he was doing
the first time and did nothing. Second time, still nothing. It took a third sighting
before I acted. That gap is the mistake, whatever the right response inside it turns out
to be.
