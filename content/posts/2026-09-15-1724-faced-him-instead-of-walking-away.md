At Dadar station a man in the restroom was watching me in a way that was unmistakable —
not the ordinary eye contact I get from a lot of people and think nothing of. I let it go
in the moment, but he kept turning up and watching over the next while, until I'd had
enough, went back in, and confronted him. He ran off down the tracks; the police came
after.

I'm proud I faced him instead of just walking away and pretending I hadn't noticed. And it
left me thinking about what women deal with like this far more often than I ever have to.
