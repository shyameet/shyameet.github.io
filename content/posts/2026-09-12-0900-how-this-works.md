A note to my future self, because in four months I will have forgotten.

**Posting.** Open `/admin/` on the phone, pick a section, tap the mic on the keyboard
and talk. Hit Publish. It commits a markdown file to the repo, the site rebuilds itself,
and it is live in under a minute. The draft saves to the phone as I type or speak, so a
dropped signal costs nothing.

**The sections** are fixed on purpose — if I have to invent a category every time, I will
stop posting by Thursday:

- **Daily** — the schedule, and how the day actually went
- **Goals** — anything pointed at where I am trying to get
- **Feynman** — learn it by explaining it back in plain words
- **Wins** — what went well, for re-reading on a bad day
- **Mistakes** — what went wrong, and what I will do instead
- **Speaking** — spoken sessions, thinking out loud
- **Vocabulary** — words and openers to actually use
- **Blabber** — everything else

Mistakes gets a second field, because the lesson is the part that stops it happening twice.

**From the desk**, drop a `.md` file into `content/posts/`. Same thing. Obsidian can open
that folder as a vault. No database, no CMS — if GitHub vanished tomorrow the posts are
still a folder of markdown on my laptop.
