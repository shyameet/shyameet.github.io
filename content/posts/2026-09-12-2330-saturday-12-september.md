Woke up at 10 and missed the gym. No confidence, no courage, no energy to go. Moved leg
and shoulder day to the evening instead, which is fine — and it gave me a better idea.

**New rule: on weekends the gym moves to the evening.** Saturday and Sunday mornings go to
reading instead. That is two extra hours of reading in the week, out of time I was losing
anyway.

## Morning

Stretched after getting up. Read the newspaper. Breakfast was paneer and aloo, fried, with
jalapeño sauce.

Then work — with the usual timepass in the middle of it. Songs, videos, looking around.

Applied for a part-time job in prediction markets. Emailed the guy and DM'd him on
LinkedIn. Nothing back yet.

Did some work on this website. Not much on the strategies, and nothing yet on what to
prepare for this week. Mostly chilling, honestly.

## Afternoon

Dal and rice. Then an episode of *Billions*, which turned into a nap of about an hour and
a half, until five.

Freshened up and went back to work — this website again, and the analysis for the week.

I cannot remember much of what I actually did. That is the reason for this: what I did
yesterday, what I need to do today, what is coming tomorrow. Written down, so it exists.

## Gym — legs and shoulders

Went in the evening as planned. Not much rough work, but:

- Lateral raises, side lateral raises, rows for shoulders
- Core: crunches, one-arm dumbbell work
- Only two leg exercises. Too few — should have been at least three.
- Some boxing at the end

Getting back into it. I want the stamina up.

## Walking Rocco

Met Shivam, Hassan and Nirud on the way.

Then a drunk man, and a lucky escape. Rocco had spotted a small-breed dog on a leash — a
poodle, I think — and gone into aggressive mode. That is the pattern with him and I still
cannot work it out: he is completely chill with stray dogs and even with stray cats, but
a dog **on a leash** and he goes mad.

While he was like that, the drunk man tried to touch him. Rocco didn't give him much
attention, which is the only reason it ended fine. I was genuinely afraid he was going to
bite him, and that would have been a whole situation.

**The thing that worked:** when the leashed dog came back round, I picked Rocco up. That
is the trick with him — once he is off the ground he has to concentrate on balancing, and
his attention is just gone. He forgets everything. So when he is aggressive or being
naughty, pick him up and hold him until the other dog passes.

## Evening

Home. Some work on the computer, the recap of the day, caught up on WhatsApp. Dinner was
palak paneer, oily chapatis, milk and oats.

## Crime and Punishment

Then almost an hour of reading a book, which I have not done in a long time — *Crime and
Punishment*, Fyodor Dostoevsky. The whole hour went on the introduction, which is very
long. It covers the author, his life, and what the book contains.

What I remember of it: a man living in Petersburg, poor, with a mother and a sister. There
is a landlady, a bad one, who mistreats her own sister. He decides she does not deserve to
live — that the world would be better off without evil people like her — and plans to
murder her.

The opposite of what he expected happens. After the murder he cannot live with himself.
The tension, the guilt, the depression that comes after it. He goes to the police and
confesses on his own. This is the 1800s, so getting away with it would have been easy and
catching the killer hard — and he still hands himself in.

That is as far as I got.
