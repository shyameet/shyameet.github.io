I didn't plan properly how to meet Rahul's parents at the airport. I thought I could reach
in time, and I didn't. So I had two gifts, around ₹3,300–3,500 together, and couldn't give
them.

The money isn't wasted — I'll give them the gifts in the future. What was wasted was my
thinking that I could just make it. Rahul said it right in that moment, and it's the lesson
below in his words.
