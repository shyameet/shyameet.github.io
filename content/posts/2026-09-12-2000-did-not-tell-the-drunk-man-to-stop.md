title: "Did not tell the drunk man to stop"
date: 2026-09-12T20:00:00+05:30
section: mistakes
lesson: "Say it plainly and immediately — \"do not touch him, he is aggressive right now\". It is not rudeness, it is the warning the situation actually needed."
tags: [people]
---

Rocco was already in aggressive mode over a leashed dog when a drunk man reached out to
touch him. I said nothing. I was afraid, or I did not have the courage — either way I
stood there while a stranger put his hand towards a dog I knew was about to go off.

It ended fine only because Rocco ignored him. That is luck, not handling it. If he had
bitten the man it would have been a serious situation, and it would have been mine to
deal with.

The same thing that stops me speaking to people is what stopped me here. It was not even
a confrontation — it was a warning, and the man needed it more than I did.
