Monday was a big one — a lot of good in it and a lot I am still turning over. The separate
pieces have their own entries; this is just the shape of the day, start to finish.

Hesitant about the gym for no real reason, but went anyway — sattu and a banana first,
chest and triceps. Figured out mid-session that my chest work isn't emphasizing the chest
enough the way I'm doing it, and I need more core in the mix too. Breakfast after: Maggi
and roti.

Then to the office in my gaon, because I'd told Rahul I'd be there Monday for work, and I
was. We got into the state of the markets and a volume-based filter idea for entries — the
full version of that is its own post, but the short version is I backtested it and it
didn't hold up.

From there, the day turned into visiting friends with Ganpati at home — Ashwin, Sanket,
Mrigang, Ruturaj. Route was Naigaon to Dadar by train, and Dadar is where the thing with
the man in the station restroom happened — also its own entries, one win and one I'm still
sitting with.

Train from Dadar to Sion, where Savan and Satyam met me. A random auto to Ashwin's, where
Sanket was already waiting. From there Sanket drove us to Powai for Mrigang's — good time,
but also where I noticed something about how differently I was received compared to a year
ago, which is its own entry too.

Last stop was Ruturaj's in Thane. Lucky on traffic — the whole of Mumbai was strangely
empty, so the cross-city trip cost us nothing. Talking with Ruturaj's dad there is where I
ran into the conversation problem, also written up separately.

Last mistake of the day: we went to find dinner for Satyam and Ruturaj afterward, which
ran long and pushed everything back. If we'd just gone home we'd have been back by 12. I
still enjoyed the later part of the night, for what it's worth — including finding out
there are trains that run late just to reach the yard where they're stored for the night,
and people were still boarding them anyway, for no reason I could figure out.

Got home very late. That lateness is most of why today felt the way it did.
