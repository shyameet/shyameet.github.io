After Ruturaj's we went to sort out dinner for Satyam and Ruturaj, which ran long and
pushed everything back. Straight home instead, and I'd have been back by 12 and asleep at
a reasonable hour. That one detour is most of why today was a write-off — I did enjoy the
extra time in the moment, but it's a straight line from that decision to how flat today
felt.
