Let my emotions take over again instead of just saying the honest thing to someone's
face. I should have said it straight. Instead I tried to be someone I am not, and stayed
quiet on what I actually meant.

The mistake underneath it is always the same one: trying to change myself for someone who
is not really me in the first place.
