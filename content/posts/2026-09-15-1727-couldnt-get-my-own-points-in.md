Talking with Ruturaj's dad, he just kept going, and I couldn't find a way to place my own
points in front of him. I don't think the fix is interrupting him — letting someone finish
is right. The part I actually got wrong is what happened after: I let his momentum carry
the whole conversation instead of steering it back once he paused. Same muscle as the
talk-to-anyone-confidently goal, just showing up in a specific room with a specific person.
