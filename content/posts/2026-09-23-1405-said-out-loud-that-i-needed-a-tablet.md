At the end of the coffee I told Rahul I need a tablet — for the work, and for my own
study. He said he'll gift it to me on my birthday, 22 October.

Very good that I said it. I was about to order one on EMI or some other way, because it's
really needed. I can't depend on notebooks forever — I'm clumsy, I forget my books, my pen.
With a tablet I'm not tied to sitting at the laptop or the desktop again and again.

Whatever is going on in our lives feels interconnected in some way. I don't have a proper
reason why it happens like that.
